
## We will discuss 4 method to get out of VIM

### Method 1

1. click-> `Esc`
2. click-> `:` 
3. click-> `w`
4. click-> `q`
5. click-> `Enter`

<img src="https://imgur.com/PABrjD9.png" width="400px" height="200px" alt="img"/>

### Method 2
1. click-> `Esc`
2. click-> `:`
3. click-> `x`
4. click-> `Enter`

<img src="https://imgur.com/UyveLyz.png" width="400px" height="200px" alt="img"/>

### Method 3

If you just want to exit without saving the content

1. click-> `Esc`
2. click-> `q`
3. click -> `!`
4. click-> `Enter`
